PCB Development · Stage 02

Advanced 4-Layer ESP32 SLAM PCB

A production-oriented 4-layer design proposal that carries the first board's field lessons into a compact controller featuring an onboard ESP32-WROOM-32, USB-C programming, and dedicated ground/power planes.

Status
Validated DFM Design Proposal
Dimensions
100.8 × 65.0 mm Compact
Stack-Up
4-Layer JLC04161H-7628 FR-4
Tools
KiCad 8, DFM Checks, iBOM

What changed from the first PCB

The implemented 2-layer board proved the system architecture, but it still depended on a bulky ESP32 devkit and discrete breakout boards. This second design explored how the robot controller could become significantly more compact, electrically robust, and production-ready.

I transitioned directly to the bare ESP32-WROOM-32 surface-mount module, integrated an onboard USB-C interface with CH340C UART bridge and automatic bootloader switching, implemented onboard low-noise LDO regulators, and segregated signal routes onto external layers while dedicating internal layers 2 and 3 to solid ground and power planes.

“Moving from 2 to 4 layers with dedicated internal planes cut electromagnetic interference from the high-current DC motor drives and reduced board surface area by over 35%.”

Integrated control

ESP32-WROOM-32 module replaces the full breakout board.

Onboard programming

USB-C with CH340C and automatic reset/boot circuits for direct firmware flashing.

Layer discipline

High-speed signals isolated from internal solid power and ground planes.

Reduced envelope

Smaller form factor frees up internal chassis volume for larger battery packs.
