Engineering Philosophy

My practical background connects multi-layer PCB design in KiCad, parametric CAD modeling in SolidWorks, embedded firmware in C/C++, and industrial automation experience gained at MAS Capital.

Rather than treating hardware as disconnected silos, I develop platforms from the component level up. When building the ToF SLAM autonomous mobile robot, the chassis geometry was designed in tandem with the sensor field of view, the wheel center of gravity, and the 4-layer control PCB envelope.

During my industrial training at MAS Capital, I learned firsthand that engineering excellence requires more than functional breadboards—it requires structured production BOMs, design for manufacture (DFM), strict safety interlocks, and rigorous factory floor commissioning alongside technicians and operators.
