Sense-Oil LevelGuard: Oil Monitoring

A non-invasive retrofit oil-level monitoring system engineered for industrial sewing machines, combining MS5837 hydrostatic pressure measurement, a custom ATmega328P control board, dynamic threshold alerts, and an industrial-grade enclosure.

“Manual oil checks in textile factories are intermittent and prone to human error. Sense-Oil continuous monitoring protects multi-thousand dollar machinery without requiring machine redesign.”

Processor ATmega328P Custom Control PCB

MAS Intimates Line Upgrade · 2025 · 5 min read

FLOD Configurable Glue-Pellet Hopper

A reverse-engineered replacement hopper developed for an automated adhesive extrusion line, incorporating parametric SolidWorks parts with design configurations, laser-cut inspection windows, and an integrated motor-driven clearing mechanism to prevent material bridging.

“Production stoppages due to pellet bridging cost valuable line throughput. The replacement hopper introduced smooth internal funnels and active mechanical agitation within the exact original envelope.”
