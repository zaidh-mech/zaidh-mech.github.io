FLOD Glue-Pellet Hopper

A configurable replacement hopper developed around an existing Flat Line On Demand machine to improve adhesive pellet flow, eliminate bridging, and add active motorized clearing.

Context
MAS Intimates / Unichela
Period
2025
Role
Reverse engineering, CAD & integration
Tools
SolidWorks Configurations, 3D printing, controls

Why it was designed

The existing FLOD machine needed a more dependable way to deliver hot-melt adhesive pellets into its screw and extrusion feeding system.

Production trials showed that inconsistent flow and pellet blockage could interrupt feeding. The replacement had to fit the existing machine without altering the supplier's core equipment, remain light enough for practical operator handling, and be straightforward to fabricate, commission and maintain.

“Production stoppages due to pellet bridging cost valuable line throughput. The replacement hopper introduced smooth internal funnels and active mechanical agitation within the exact original envelope.”

Consistent feeding

Guide pellets toward the screw inlet with fewer stagnant areas and a smoother outlet transition.

Machine compatibility

Preserve the existing mounting envelope and interfaces instead of redesigning the FLOD machine.

Visual monitoring

Allow operators to check pellet levels quickly without opening the hopper lid during production.

Active clearing

Integrate a compact agitator to clear bridging automatically without adding bulk or high power demands.

Design requirements

The hopper was developed against functional constraints derived directly from factory floor operation.

  • 01Maintain the physical mounting footprint, screw clearance and feed height.
  • 02Provide smooth internal funnel transitions to prevent pellet stagnation.
  • 03Integrate transparent inspection windows for fast optical level checks.
  • 04Fit an internal motor-driven agitator mechanism to break bridging.
  • 05Ensure tool-less access to internal components for rapid routine cleaning.

SolidWorks Modeling

Parametric configurations for adaptable capacity.

The hopper body was built using SolidWorks configurations to support varying machine heights and production volume demands while retaining common mounting features.
